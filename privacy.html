Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Gold-Price.app is a free, informational website that displays live precious metal prices. We do not require account registration and do not collect personally identifiable information directly.

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data (pages visited, approximate location by country, device type, referring site) to help us understand how visitors use the site and improve it. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you.

2. Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*) to distinguish unique visitors. These cookies do not contain personally identifiable information. You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics entirely with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site works fully with cookies disabled.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google Analytics (Google LLC) for anonymous usage analytics and TradingView widgets to display price charts. These providers may set their own cookies subject to their privacy policies.

4. Data Retention

Google Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings. No personal data is stored on our own servers.

5. Your Rights (EU/UK/California)

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA, you have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Since we collect only anonymous analytics data, there is typically no personally identifiable information to retrieve. To request any action, contact us at hello@gold-price.app.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, email hello@gold-price.app.
